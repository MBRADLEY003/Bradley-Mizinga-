Actuarial science · Software

Where actuarial thinking meets software

My background is in actuarial science: the discipline of pricing uncertainty, modelling loss, and turning noisy data into a number someone can actually defend. Over the past year I've been applying that same thinking to software, building fraud-detection and forecasting tools for logistics fleets using z-scores, moving averages, and Monte Carlo methods rather than guesswork.

I'm drawn to problems that sit between two fields most people keep separate. A statistics background changes how you'd design a product in the first place, and writing the software yourself changes how deeply you can test the statistics. That combination is what I'm building toward.

What is AXIOM?

Intelligent Fleet & Logistics Intelligence

AXIOM

From transport data to operational intelligence.

AXIOM is an intelligent transport and fleet analytics platform built to help organisations understand what is happening across their fleet. It brings together operational data such as fuel usage, kilometres, vehicle activity and driver information to identify patterns, highlight unusual events and support better operational decisions.

DATA→ANALYSIS→ RISK→INSIGHT→ACTION
